About Us

Obada Alshammat

Founder of WingSpan Fitness

I’m a 17-year-old entrepreneur from Sweden and the founder of WingSpan Fitness, established on January 25, 2026. What started as a personal passion for training quickly evolved into a vision to help others build strength, discipline, and confidence.

WingSpan Fitness is more than just a brand it represents growth, ambition, and the mindset of pushing beyond limits. My goal is to inspire people to take control of their physical and mental development.
